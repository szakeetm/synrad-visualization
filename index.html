A lab for building intuition

This is a teaching companion to Synrad tutorial by Márton Ady and Gabriel Saretti (CERN), CAS Prague, 16 November 2026. Slide numbers refer to the 44-page lecture supplied with this project.

The spectrum and vertical polarization use numerical integrals of modified Bessel functions. Reference-frame ray directions follow relativistic aberration. The horizontal fan is a uniform bend convolved with a Gaussian approximation to the emission cone.

Material results are illustrative: free-electron grazing Fresnel reflection and an E⁻³ attenuation law omit absorption edges, real optical tables, fluorescence and scattering. The rough-surface fan is qualitative. Insertion-device spectra illustrate finite-train interference and a smooth transition to a broad wiggler envelope; they are not a beamline calculation.

All geometry and animation speeds are schematic. Values in the controls and plots carry the physical units. These models are intended for learning.
